Sage-A1.5：把编排与自校验写进 35B 智能体策略

Sage-A1.5: From Long-Horizon Execution to Scientific Exploration with Capability Composition · Wenjie Lou, Tianshuo Peng, Shiyang Feng, Shengji Tang, Runmin Ma, et al. (large consortium)

FALCA：用证伪智能体打断深度搜索的确认偏误

FALCA: Falsification-Aware Reinforcement Learning with Hierarchical Credit Assignment for Long-Horizon Deep Search Agents · Zijun Dou, Kun Luo, Chaofan Li, Shuqi Lu, Zheng Liu

把深度搜索写成 propose–falsify：Searcher 提议，独立 Falsifier 找反证与未满足约束并回写研究状态；训练含证伪轨迹 SFT＋并行 rollout 的分层信用分配。FALCA-4B 五基准均分约 64.7，高于同 harness 未训的 Qwen3.5-4B（55.5）与 9B（57.9）；去 Falsifier 掉到 52.8。结论：搜得更深不够，还得会改研究状态。
